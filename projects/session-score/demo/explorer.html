Session feed

The Mac app reads local Codex session logs through its Electron process. Verified work starts playback. The last session to finish releases the music. The browser demo supplies a fictional session with three tool events.

Action rules and presets

The classifier turns a named tool into a read, edit or command. Choose Classical, Electronic or Hyperpop in the demo. The desktop mixer also lets you assign instruments, pitch, volume and phrase lengths per action.

Bass melody and arrangement

One bass line sets the key and tempo. Actions queue keys, chords or drums for a bar boundary. The app includes ten bass melodies and can hold one or cycle through them.

Synthesis and audio output

The Web Audio engine synthesizes each note and mixes the layers. Normal completion fades sounding notes. Stop the demo to silence it immediately.
